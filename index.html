Frequently Asked Questions

Do you work on European / import vehicles?

Yes — European, Asian, and domestic makes are our daily bread. We invest in factory-level diagnostic tools for VAG (VW, Audi, Škoda, Seat), BMW, Mercedes-Benz, and PSA groups, and our technicians hold brand-specific training. Hybrid and EV platforms are covered too, with high-voltage-certified staff.

Will servicing my car here void my new car factory warranty?

No. Under EU consumer law (and the Magnuson-Moss Warranty Act in the US), manufacturers cannot require you to service at their dealerships. We follow your manufacturer's official service schedule, use OEM or OEM-equivalent parts, and stamp your digital service book — your warranty stays fully intact.

Do I need an appointment, or do you accept walk-ins?

Appointments get priority and guaranteed bay time, so we strongly recommend booking online or calling ahead. That said, we keep capacity for walk-ins every day — oil changes, battery tests, and check engine light scans can often be handled same-day. Emergencies are always triaged first.

Do you offer financing for major repairs?

Yes. For repairs over 1,000 zł we offer interest-free installment plans (3–6 months) and accept Synchrony Car Care for extended financing. Approval takes about five minutes at the front desk — just bring an ID. We'll always split repairs into "safety-critical now" vs. "can wait" so you can plan.
