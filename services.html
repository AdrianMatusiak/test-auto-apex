Check Engine & Computer Diagnostics

That amber light doesn't tell you what's wrong — it tells you where to look. Our factory-level scan tools read live data from every control module, so we diagnose the actual fault instead of throwing parts at a code.

  • Check engine, ABS, airbag, and transmission light diagnostics
  • Live sensor data analysis and module communication tests
  • Electrical fault tracing: parasitic drains, wiring, grounds
  • Emissions readiness testing and drive-cycle completion

Brake Repair & Replacement

Brakes are the one system we never compromise on. Every brake job includes measured pad thickness, rotor runout readings, and photographed evidence in your digital inspection.

  • Pad and rotor replacement (OEM and premium aftermarket)
  • Caliper service, slide pin rebuilds, and brake hose replacement
  • Brake fluid flush with boiling-point testing
  • ABS pump and sensor diagnosis and repair
  • Parking brake adjustment and cable replacement

Factory Scheduled Maintenance & Oil Changes

We follow your manufacturer's exact service intervals using OEM-spec fluids and filters — which keeps your factory warranty fully intact and your service book stamped.

  • Full synthetic oil changes with OEM-spec filters
  • Interval services: 15k, 30k, 60k, 90k, 120k km packages
  • Cabin and engine air filters, fuel filters, spark plugs
  • Coolant, transmission, brake, and differential fluid services
  • Timing belt / chain replacement per manufacturer schedule
  • Complimentary 40-point inspection with every service visit

AC, Heating & Cooling Systems

Warm in January, cold in July. We service the full climate system, from refrigerant to heater cores, with proper recovery and recycling equipment.

  • AC performance testing and refrigerant recharge (R134a / R1234yf)

  • Radiator, thermostat, water pump, and cooling fan repair
  • Heater core and blend-door service
